Story

Four things worth knowing before you read the code.

This is the personal account. Every project page below is the technical one.

Who I am

I am the person who owns the whole system.

I am a B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science in Chennai. What I like about software is the part after the idea: choosing the boundaries, deciding what should be deterministic, and writing the thing so it still works when the happy path runs out. On this site each project carries its own architecture, its engineering decisions, and what did not work — because that is the part I actually want to be judged on.

What I like building

Systems where software, data and AI have to cooperate.

A multimodal verification pipeline that keeps its evidence traceable. A crime intelligence platform where a risk score has to be explainable to the person reading it. A road intelligence system running inference on a Raspberry Pi instead of a data centre. Different domains, same shape of problem: several layers that only work if the interfaces between them are right.

How I think

Start with the problem, and make the trade-offs explicit.

AI is an option among several, not the default answer. A deterministic rule beats a model when the problem is deterministic. I would rather write the simplest thing that works and be able to say exactly why each part exists. When something fails, that is information about my design, not bad luck.

Why I build

Because I want to be able to explain every line.

I would rather ship three systems I fully understand than ten I half remember. This site is the same instinct applied to the pages: the work, the decisions, the results and the gaps, all in one place, written by the person who built them.

Thinking

Four principles I keep coming back to.

They decide what I build, how I build it, and when I refuse to add another layer.

  1. 01Start with the problem.
  2. 02Make trade-offs explicit.
  3. 03Prefer the simplest solution that works.
  4. 04Use AI when it creates leverage.
